# PROMPTS.md — Veo3 prompty pro video Previo

> **Formát:** 9:16 vertical | **Délka celkem:** 15s | **6 záběrů (v2)**  
> Každý prompt je samostatný Veo3 input. Generovat postupně, záběr po záběru.

---

## ZÁBĚR 1 — Hook / Chaos (0–2s)

```
Vertical 9:16 video. A stressed female hotel receptionist, late 20s to late 30s, Central European appearance, light skin, natural brown or dark blonde hair pulled back, wearing a slightly disheveled white button-up shirt, stands behind a wooden hotel front desk. A landline desk phone is ringing loudly — she grabs it quickly. At the same moment she's holding a messy stack of paper reservation sheets in her other hand, visibly overwhelmed. In the background, slightly out of focus, an impatient middle-aged male guest, Central European appearance, stands at the desk waiting with arms crossed, checking his watch. Her expression is stressed but restrained — furrowed brow, tight jaw, not theatrical. Real Central European hotel lobby, warm wooden interior, not a studio set. Natural window light from the left. Handheld camera with slight shake, fast energy. Shot duration 2 seconds. Cut mid-motion. Lifestyle film aesthetic, muted warm tones, no color filters.
```

## ZÁBĚR 2 — Bolest / Papírový diář (2–5s)

```
Vertical 9:16 video. Extreme close-up over-the-shoulder shot looking down at a handwritten paper reservation diary on a wooden hotel front desk. A female receptionist's hand, light skin, unpolished natural nails, rapidly flips through pages — fingers scanning lines of handwritten names and dates, pausing, flipping back, unable to find the entry. The pages are dense with cramped handwriting, crossings-out, sticky note flags. A second hand taps the desk impatiently. Warm desk lamp light from the right, creating a slight shadow across the open pages. Very shallow depth of field — diary sharp, background lobby completely blurred. Camera very slightly drifts forward over 3 seconds. No face visible, just hands and paper. Handheld, micro-jitter. Lifestyle documentary aesthetic, unglamorous and real.
```

## ZÁBĚR 3 — Zvrat / Previo tablet (5–8s)

```
Vertical 9:16 video. A female hotel receptionist, late 20s to late 30s, Central European appearance, light skin, natural brown or dark blonde hair, wearing a light blue or white shirt, standing at a tidy wooden hotel front desk. She taps a white iPad tablet with her index finger. As she looks at the screen her shoulders visibly drop and relax — a physical release of tension, a quiet exhale. A soft, genuine smile. The tablet screen shows a clean hotel room occupancy grid dashboard with colored blocks. Camera at chest height slightly to her right, framing both her face and the tablet in the same vertical shot. Soft natural window light from the left. The mood shifts: from tight to open, from chaos to ease. Shot is 3 seconds. Very slow smooth push-in toward the tablet screen. Lifestyle film, warm neutral tones, authentic. Not commercial.
```

## ZÁBĚR 4 — Systém funguje / Komunikace bez chaosu (8–11s)

```
Vertical 9:16 video. Close-up over-the-shoulder shot of a female hotel receptionist, late 20s to late 30s, Central European appearance, light skin, dark blonde hair, wearing a light shirt. She is sitting calmly at the hotel front desk, looking at a desktop monitor or large tablet screen. The screen shows a unified inbox or communication dashboard — multiple channels (chat bubbles, email icons, booking confirmations) all visible and organized in a clean interface. She clicks through items with calm, practiced ease — one tap to confirm a reservation, one tap to reply to a message. Her posture is relaxed, upright. No phone ringing, no papers. The desk is clear. Soft ambient office light, window light from the left. Camera slightly behind her right shoulder, drifting slowly left. The energy is flow, not rush. Shot is 3 seconds. Lifestyle film aesthetic, warm neutral tones.
```

## ZÁBĚR 5 — Majitel hotelu — v kontrole (11–13s)

```
Vertical 9:16 video. A relaxed male hotel owner, early 40s to mid 50s, Central European appearance, light skin, short dark or slightly greying hair, wearing a casual smart dark merino sweater. He is standing in a hotel corridor or lobby — wooden interior, soft lighting, hotel key rack or reception desk softly visible in the background establishing the location. He holds a white iPhone in one hand at waist level, glances down at the screen showing a clean mobile occupancy dashboard, then looks up with a calm, quiet nod of satisfaction. Expression: in control, no stress, quiet confidence. Restrained acting — small nod, not a big smile. Morning light from a window at the end of the corridor, gentle fill light on his face. Shot is 2 seconds. Slow handheld drift forward, very subtle. Lifestyle aesthetic — real person, not actor.
```

## ZÁBĚR 6 — CTA text overlay (13–15s)

> Text overlay přidán v editaci — není potřeba Veo3 ani motion design card.

**Text k zobrazení:**
- Logo Previo (nahoře nebo uprostřed)
- Řádek 1: `Dáváme vám svobodu.`
- Řádek 2: `previo.cz`

**Doporučení pro editaci:**
- Pořadí: logo → text pod ním
- Pozice: střed nebo dolní třetina
- Barva: bílá na tmavém podkladu, nebo `#b50000` červená na světlém
- Font: semibold, čistý sans-serif
- Animace: logo fade-in, text fade-in s mírným zpožděním (0.3s)

---

## Audio / Hudba — popis pro celé video

```
Background music: lo-fi ambient instrumental. Slow tempo, 70–80 BPM. No percussion drop, no uplift moment. Piano or acoustic guitar with subtle ambient pad. Slightly melancholic in the first 6 seconds (chaos section), gently resolving in the last 9 seconds (calm section). No brand jingle. Audio mix: music at -18dB under voiceover. Voiceover is warm, natural male or female Czech voice, conversational pace, not announcer style.
```
